MADE BY ATHARV TRIPATHI

🏝️ ISLAND
SURVIVOR

You've washed up on a wild island. Gather wood, stone and berries by day. Build walls, campfires and spike traps before dark — every night the monsters come in bigger waves. Survive 10 nights until the rescue ship arrives.

MOVE: WASD / arrows · ACTION (gather / attack): SPACE or J · EAT: E
BUILD: pick an item below (or keys 1-4), then click a nearby tile — or press F to place it in front of you
Campfires heal you, light the dark and burn monsters that get close. Refuel a fire with ACTION (2 wood).
Phones: joystick to move · ACT button · tap an item, then tap a tile
