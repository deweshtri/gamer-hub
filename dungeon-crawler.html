🗡️ DUNGEON
CRAWLER

Descend 9 floors of a randomly generated dungeon. Fight monsters, loot weapons and armor, and slay the bosses guarding floors 3, 6 and 9. When you fall, you keep half your gold for permanent upgrades at camp.

MOVE / ATTACK: arrow keys or WASD — walk into a monster to hit it · tap a side of the map on phones
Q: drink potion · SPACE: wait a turn · step on the stairs ▼ to go deeper
Big boss attacks flash red first — step out of the red tiles!
